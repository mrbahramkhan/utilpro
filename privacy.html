Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

1. Introduction

UtilPro ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website at utilpro.net.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our site, we may automatically collect standard web log information such as your IP address, browser type, referring/exit pages, and date/time stamps. This is collected through Google Analytics.

2.2 Tool Inputs

Inputs you enter into our online tools (dates, amounts, passwords, etc.) are processed entirely in your browser. They are never sent to our servers and are not stored anywhere.

2.3 Cookies

We use cookies through Google AdSense and Google Analytics. These are third-party cookies used for analytics and advertising personalization. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

3. Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads.

4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Analytics data is anonymized and used only to improve our content. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Data Security

Our tools run client-side in your browser. The Password Generator, for example, generates passwords locally and never transmits them to any server. We do not store any personal tool inputs.

6. Third-Party Links

Our guides may link to third-party websites (government portals, educational resources, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

7. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: privacy@utilpro.net or use our Contact page.
