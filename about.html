About UtilPro

Free tools and guides for everyone — built with simplicity in mind.

What is UtilPro?

UtilPro is a free utility website that provides everyday online tools, calculators, converters, and step-by-step problem-solving guides. Our goal is simple: help people solve common problems quickly, without ads popups, without logins, and without fees.

Our Tools

We offer 10 fully working online tools including an Age Calculator, Loan EMI Calculator, BMI Calculator, Percentage Calculator, Password Generator, Currency Converter, Unit Converter, Time Zone Converter, Date Difference Calculator, and PDF Tools — all free, all instant, all mobile-friendly.

Our Guides

We publish verified, step-by-step guides across four categories:

  • Mobile Fix — WhatsApp, Android, Instagram, Facebook issues
  • Govt Services (Pakistan) — CNIC, BISP, Passport, Electricity & Gas bills
  • Education — BISE results, past papers, scholarships, admissions
  • Tech Fix — Windows, WiFi, phone, app problems
