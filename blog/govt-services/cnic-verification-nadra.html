CNIC verification is needed for bank accounts, jobs, government services, and SIM registration. NADRA provides three official ways to verify a CNIC online in Pakistan.

⚡ Fastest: Send your 13-digit CNIC number via SMS to 7000 — get instant verification in under 60 seconds.

Method 1: Verify via SMS to 7000

1

Open SMS app

Open the default messaging app on your mobile phone.

2

Type 13-digit CNIC number

Type your CNIC without dashes. Example: 3520112345678

3

Send to 7000

Send the SMS to 7000. You'll receive a reply confirming CNIC validity. Works on all networks.

✅ SMS fee: approx. Rs. 2-3. Works on Jazz, Zong, Telenor, Ufone.

Method 2: Verify via NADRA Website

1

Visit nadra.gov.pk

Open your browser and go to the official NADRA website: nadra.gov.pk or id.nadra.gov.pk

2

Click "Verify ID"

Find and click the "Verify ID" or "CNIC Verification" option in the menu.

3

Enter CNIC and CAPTCHA

Enter the 13-digit CNIC number and complete the CAPTCHA verification.

4

View results

The system shows whether the CNIC is valid, expired, or not found.

Method 3: NADRA eSahulat App

Download the official NADRA eSahulat app from Google Play or App Store. After registration you can verify CNICs, apply for renewal, book appointments, and pay fees online.

Track CNIC Application Status

  • SMS: Send tracking ID to 8400
  • Online: track.nadra.gov.pk
  • Helpline: 051-111-786-100

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is CNIC verification free?

Website verification is free. SMS costs standard rate (Rs. 2-3).

Q: How long is a CNIC valid?

CNICs are valid for 10 years. Renew before expiry to avoid issues.

Q: My CNIC shows invalid?

Visit your nearest NADRA office with original documents. It may need renewal or re-registration.

⚠️ Only use official NADRA channels. Third-party CNIC verification websites may be scams.
