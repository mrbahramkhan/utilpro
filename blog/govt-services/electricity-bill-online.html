How to Check Electricity Bill Online in Pakistan — All DISCOs (2026)

📅 June 2026⏱ 3 min read🇵🇰 Pakistan
📢 Advertisement

Pakistan has 9 electricity distribution companies (DISCOs). You can check your electricity bill online in seconds by entering your 14-digit reference number. Here's how for each DISCO.

⚡ Quick tip: Your reference number is printed on your electricity bill, usually at the top right. It is 14 digits long.

How to Check Bill Online (Step by Step)

1

Find your DISCO

Check the table above to identify which DISCO serves your area.

2

Visit the DISCO website

Open the official website in your browser (e.g., lesco.gov.pk for Lahore).

3

Click "Check Bill" or "Duplicate Bill"

Most DISCO websites have a prominent "Bill Inquiry" or "Duplicate Bill" button on the homepage.

4

Enter your 14-digit reference number

Your reference number (also called Consumer Number or Account Number) is on your physical bill. Enter it and click Submit.

5

View and download your bill

Your current bill details will appear. You can print or download it as a PDF.

Pay Electricity Bill Online

After checking your bill, you can pay it through:

  • JazzCash: App → Pay Bills → Electricity → Select DISCO → Enter reference number
  • Easypaisa: App → Bill Payments → Electricity → Select company
  • Internet Banking: HBL, UBL, MCB, Meezan online portals
  • 1Link: Any ATM with 1Link service
  • Bank branch: Pay at any bank or DISCO payment center

Download Duplicate Electricity Bill

If you've lost your original bill, visit your DISCO's website, enter your reference number, and click "Print Duplicate Bill" or "Download PDF." The duplicate bill is accepted for payments at banks.

✅ Tip: Save your DISCO website link as a bookmark for quick monthly bill checks. Set up auto-pay through your bank to avoid late payment surcharges.
