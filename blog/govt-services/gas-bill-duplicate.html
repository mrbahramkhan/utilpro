Download Duplicate Gas Bill Online — SNGPL & SSGCL Pakistan (2026)

📅 June 2026⏱ 3 min read🇵🇰 Pakistan
📢 Advertisement

Pakistan has two gas distribution companies: SNGPL (Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited — Punjab & KPK) and SSGCL (Sui Southern Gas Company Limited — Sindh & Balochistan). Here's how to download your duplicate bill from each.

SNGPL Duplicate Gas Bill (Punjab & KPK)

1

Visit sngpl.com.pk

Go to the official SNGPL website: sngpl.com.pk

2

Click "Online Services" → "Duplicate Bill"

Look for Online Services or Customer Services menu and select Duplicate Bill.

3

Enter your Consumer Number

Your 10-digit Consumer Number (also called Account Number) is on your physical gas bill, usually on the top right.

4

Download PDF bill

Your current bill will be displayed. Click "Print" or "Download PDF" to save it.

SSGCL Duplicate Gas Bill (Sindh & Balochistan)

1

Visit ssgc.com.pk

Go to ssgc.com.pk — the official Sui Southern Gas Company website.

2

Select "Duplicate Bill"

Find it under Customer Services or the main menu.

3

Enter Consumer Number

Enter your consumer/account number from your previous bill or meter booklet.

Pay Gas Bill Online

  • JazzCash App: Pay Bills → Gas → Select SNGPL or SSGCL → Enter consumer number
  • Easypaisa App: Bill Payments → Gas → Enter details
  • Bank apps: HBL, UBL, MCB, Meezan, Bank Alfalah all support gas bill payments
  • ATM: Any ATM in Pakistan via 1Link supports gas bill payment

Common Gas Bill Problems

Bill not received this month?

Download duplicate bill online or visit your nearest gas company office. Bills not received for 2+ months may indicate meter reading issue.

Unusually high bill?

File a complaint via the gas company website or helpline. Request meter accuracy test if readings seem wrong.

Gas Company Helplines

  • SNGPL: 0800-00786 (toll-free)
  • SSGCL: 021-111-786-786
