Pakistan Passport Tracking — How to Check Status Online (2026)

📅 June 2026⏱ 3 min read🇵🇰 Pakistan
📢 Advertisement

⚡ Track online: Visit dgip.gov.pk → "Track Passport" → Enter your CNIC or token number.

How to Track Passport Status Online

1

Visit official DGIP website

Go to passport.gov.pk or dgip.gov.pk (Directorate General of Immigration & Passports)

2

Click "Track Passport"

Find the "Track Your Passport" or "Passport Status" option on the homepage.

3

Enter your CNIC or token number

Enter your 13-digit CNIC number or the token/reference number you received when submitting your application.

4

View current status

The portal will show your passport status: In Process, Ready for Collection, Dispatched, or Delivered.

Passport Status Messages Explained

  • Application Received — Your form has been accepted at the passport office
  • In Process / Under Verification — Being reviewed; NADRA verifying your details
  • Printed — Passport has been printed at the printing press
  • Dispatched — Sent to your passport office or home via TCS courier
  • Ready for Collection — Available at your passport office; bring CNIC
  • Delivered — Delivered to your home address (for urgent/online applications)

✅ For urgent passport within 24–48 hours, visit your regional passport office in person with proof of emergency travel (ticket, visa, medical emergency).
