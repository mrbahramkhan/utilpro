How to Check BISP Payment Status Online (2026)

📅 June 2026⏱ 4 min read🇵🇰 Pakistan
📢 Advertisement

Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP) is Pakistan's largest social protection initiative. Here's how to check your payment status using SMS, the official portal, and the Ehsaas app.

⚡ Quickest method: Send your CNIC number (without dashes) via SMS to 8171 to check BISP eligibility and payment status instantly.

Method 1: Check via SMS (8171)

1

Open SMS app

Open messaging on any mobile phone.

2

Type CNIC number

Type your 13-digit CNIC number without dashes. E.g.: 3310012345678

3

Send to 8171

Send to 8171. You'll receive a reply with your BISP eligibility and latest payment status.

✅ This service is free. Works on all networks 24/7.

Method 2: Check via BISP / Ehsaas Portal

1

Visit the official portal

Go to bisp.gov.pk or ehsaas.nadra.gov.pk

2

Click "Check Payment Status"

Find the payment check or beneficiary status section on the homepage.

3

Enter your CNIC

Enter your 13-digit CNIC number and complete verification.

4

View payment details

See your payment history, next payment date, and disbursement amount.

Method 3: Ehsaas App

Download the official Ehsaas app from Google Play Store or App Store. After registration with your CNIC, you can:

  • Check BISP payment status and history
  • Find nearest payment center
  • Register complaints
  • Check Ehsaas Rashan and other programmes

How to Collect BISP Payment

BISP payments are disbursed through:

  • HBL bank branches — Present your CNIC and biometric verification
  • Post offices — Available in rural areas via Pakistan Post
  • Mobile wallets — Some payments sent via Easypaisa or JazzCash
  • BISP payment centers — Dedicated centers in each district

BISP Payment 2026 — Current Installment

BISP quarterly installments are typically Rs. 10,500 per quarter for registered beneficiaries. Check the official BISP website for the latest installment amounts and schedule.

⚠️ Beware of scams: BISP never asks for money to release payments. If anyone claims to unblock your BISP payment for a fee, it is a scam. Report to 8171.

Common BISP Issues & Solutions

My name doesn't appear in BISP list?

Visit your nearest BISP office with CNIC. You can register or file a complaint for re-assessment.

Payment released but I haven't received it?

Go to the nearest HBL branch with your original CNIC. Provide biometric verification to collect.

BISP payment on hold / suspended?

This can happen if household income assessment changed. Visit BISP office with documents for re-assessment.
