Facebook Account Locked? Step-by-Step Recovery Guide (2026)

📅 June 2026⏱ 5 min read
📢 Advertisement

Facebook locks accounts for security reasons: suspicious login attempts, policy violations, or identity verification requirements. Here's how to recover your account in each scenario.

Why Facebook Locks Accounts

  • Multiple failed login attempts from different locations
  • Someone reported your account
  • Posting content that violates Community Standards
  • Your account was hacked and used for spam
  • Facebook needs to verify your identity
  • Using a fake name or multiple accounts

Fix 1: Identity Verification Lock

If Facebook asks to "Confirm your identity," you'll need to submit a government-issued ID.

1

Go to facebook.com/id

Visit this URL or tap "Continue" on the identity verification prompt.

2

Upload a clear photo ID

Submit a clear photo of your CNIC (Pakistan), Passport, or Driving License. The name must match your Facebook name.

3

Wait for review

Facebook reviews IDs within 24–48 hours. Check your registered email for updates.

Fix 2: Security Lock (Suspicious Activity)

If Facebook detected suspicious login activity, go through the security checkpoint: confirm your identity, review recent logins, change your password, and enable 2FA.

Fix 3: Account Disabled for Policy Violations

If your account was disabled, visit facebook.com/help/contact/260749603972907 to submit an appeal. In your appeal:

  • Explain you believe the disable was a mistake
  • Provide your name as shown on your account
  • Include your email address or phone number

⚠️ Facebook does not have phone support. Be cautious of anyone claiming to be "Facebook Support" — they are scammers.

Fix 4: Forgot Password

Visit facebook.com → tap "Forgot Password?" → Enter your email, phone, or name → Follow the recovery steps. If you no longer have access to the email or phone, use "No longer have access to these?" and follow the trusted contacts or ID verification route.

Fix 5: Hacked Account

If your account was hacked, go to facebook.com/hacked immediately. Facebook will walk you through securing your account, removing unauthorized apps, and reviewing posts made without your knowledge.

✅ Prevention tip: Enable two-factor authentication on Facebook: Settings → Security and Login → Two-Factor Authentication. This prevents most account hacking.
