WhatsApp OTP Not Received? 10 Proven Fixes (2026)

Not getting the WhatsApp verification code (OTP) is one of the most common problems when setting up or restoring WhatsApp. The good news is that it's almost always fixable in a few minutes. This guide covers all 10 proven fixes for both Android and iPhone.

⚡ Quick Fix First: Before trying everything below, wait 60 seconds and tap "Resend SMS". If that doesn't work, tap "Call me" to receive the code via a voice call.

Why WhatsApp OTP Doesn't Come

There are several reasons WhatsApp may not send or deliver the OTP:

  • Wrong phone number or country code entered
  • Poor mobile signal or no network connection
  • Do Not Disturb (DND) mode blocking SMS
  • Third-party SMS blocking apps installed
  • WhatsApp servers are temporarily busy
  • Your number is temporarily restricted by WhatsApp
  • SIM card not properly inserted or activated

Fix 1: Double-Check Your Phone Number

1

Verify the exact number

Make sure you've entered the correct phone number including country code. For Pakistan, use +92 followed by your 10-digit number without the leading 0.

2

Don't include leading zero

If your number is 0301-1234567, enter it as 3011234567 (without the 0). WhatsApp adds the country code (+92) automatically.

✅ Tip: On the WhatsApp number screen, tap the flag icon to make sure the correct country (+92 for Pakistan) is selected.

Fix 2: Check Mobile Signal & Network

1

Check signal bars

Make sure you have at least 2-3 signal bars. Move to an open area or near a window if indoor signal is weak.

2

Toggle Airplane Mode

Turn Airplane Mode ON for 10 seconds, then OFF. This refreshes your network connection and often resolves SMS delivery issues instantly.

3

Try calling yourself

Call any number from your SIM to confirm calls work. If calls don't work, the problem is with your network — contact your carrier.

Fix 3: Use "Resend SMS" or "Call Me" Option

1

Wait 60 seconds

WhatsApp makes you wait 60 seconds before the "Resend SMS" button appears. Wait patiently — sometimes the SMS is simply delayed.

2

Tap "Resend SMS"

After 60 seconds, tap the "Resend SMS" button. WhatsApp will send a new OTP to your number.

3

Use "Call Me" option

After another 60 seconds, a "Call me" option appears. Tap it and WhatsApp will call your phone and read out the 6-digit code.

Fix 4: Disable Do Not Disturb (DND) Mode

If DND mode is enabled, SMS messages may be silenced or blocked.

1

Android: Turn off DND

Swipe down from the top of your screen. Look for a "Do Not Disturb" or moon icon. Tap it to disable DND mode.

2

iPhone: Turn off Focus mode

Open Control Center (swipe down from top-right). Tap any active Focus mode (Do Not Disturb, Sleep, etc.) to turn it off.

Fix 5: Clear WhatsApp Cache (Android)

1

Go to Settings → Apps

Open Settings on your Android phone. Tap "Apps" or "Application Manager."

2

Find and clear WhatsApp cache

Find WhatsApp in the list. Tap "Storage" then tap "Clear Cache." Do NOT tap "Clear Data" or you'll lose your chats.

3

Reopen WhatsApp and retry

Open WhatsApp again and tap "Resend SMS" to request a new OTP.

Fix 6: Check for SMS Blocking Apps

Some apps block SMS from unknown numbers, which can prevent WhatsApp OTP delivery.

  • Check if you have a call/SMS blocking app installed (e.g., Truecaller, Mr. Number)
  • Open that app and look for a blocked numbers list
  • Whitelist messages from "WhatsApp" or temporarily disable the app

⚠️ Truecaller's spam filter sometimes blocks WhatsApp verification SMS. Open Truecaller → Settings → Block → Disable SMS blocking temporarily.

Fix 7: Update WhatsApp to the Latest Version

1

Android: Update via Play Store

Open Google Play Store → search "WhatsApp" → tap "Update" if available.

2

iPhone: Update via App Store

Open App Store → tap your profile icon → scroll down to find WhatsApp → tap "Update."

Fix 8: Reinstall WhatsApp

⚠️ Important: Backup your chats before uninstalling. Go to WhatsApp → Settings → Chats → Chat Backup → Back Up Now.

1

Uninstall WhatsApp

Long-press the WhatsApp icon → tap "Uninstall." On iPhone, long-press → "Remove App" → "Delete App."

2

Restart your phone

Restart your phone completely before reinstalling. This clears any temporary errors.

3

Reinstall from official store

Install WhatsApp from Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Do NOT use APK files from unknown sources.

Fix 9: Verify Country Code is Correct

Entering the wrong country code is a common mistake, especially for expats or people using international numbers.

  • 🇵🇰 Pakistan: +92
  • 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia: +966
  • 🇦🇪 UAE: +971
  • 🇺🇸 USA: +1
  • 🇬🇧 UK: +44
  • 🇮🇳 India: +91

Fix 10: Contact WhatsApp Support

If none of the above fixes work, your number may be temporarily banned or restricted by WhatsApp.

1

Email WhatsApp support

Send an email to support@whatsapp.com explaining the issue. Include your phone number with country code.

2

Use the in-app support

If you can open WhatsApp, go to Settings → Help → Contact Us to submit a support request directly.

3

Wait 24 hours

WhatsApp sometimes temporarily restricts OTP requests for security. Wait 24 hours and try again.

✅ Summary: Most WhatsApp OTP issues are fixed by: (1) checking your phone number, (2) toggling Airplane Mode, (3) using "Call me" option, or (4) clearing WhatsApp cache. If the problem persists for more than 24 hours, contact WhatsApp support.
