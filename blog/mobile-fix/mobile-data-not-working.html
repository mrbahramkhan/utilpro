Mobile Data Not Working? 8 Fixes for Android & iPhone (2026)

📅 June 2026⏱ 4 min read

📋 Quick Fixes

  1. Toggle Mobile Data Off/On
  2. Airplane Mode Trick
  3. Check APN Settings
  4. Check Data Limit
  5. Reseat SIM Card
  6. Select Network Manually
  7. Reset Network Settings
  8. Contact Carrier

Mobile data not working is frustrating, especially when you're away from WiFi. This guide covers all the common reasons and fixes for 4G/5G data not working on Android and iPhone.

⚡ Quickest fix: Toggle Airplane Mode on for 10 seconds, then back off. This refreshes your network connection and fixes data for 60% of cases.

Fix 1: Toggle Mobile Data Off and On

Go to Quick Settings (swipe down) and tap the Mobile Data icon to turn it off. Wait 5 seconds, then turn it back on. On iPhone: Settings → Cellular → Toggle off, wait, toggle on.

Fix 2: Airplane Mode Trick

Turn Airplane Mode ON for 15 seconds (this disconnects from all networks), then turn it OFF. Your phone will reconnect and often fix data issues automatically.

Fix 3: Check APN Settings

APN (Access Point Name) settings tell your phone how to connect to your carrier's data network. Incorrect APN settings are a very common cause of data not working.

  • Jazz/Warid Pakistan: APN: jazz, Username: jazz, Password: jazz
  • Zong Pakistan: APN: zong
  • Telenor Pakistan: APN: isp.telenor.pk
  • Ufone Pakistan: APN: ufone.pinternet

Android: Settings → Connections → Mobile Networks → Access Point Names. iPhone: Settings → Cellular → Cellular Data Network.

Fix 4: Check Your Data Balance & Limit

You may have used up your monthly data allowance. Check your balance by dialing your carrier's USSD code:

  • Jazz: *111#
  • Zong: *310#
  • Telenor: *444#
  • Ufone: *706#

Also check if you've set a data limit on your phone: Settings → Network → Data Usage → Data Limit.

Fix 5: Reseat Your SIM Card

Turn off your phone completely. Remove the SIM card, wait 10 seconds, reinsert it firmly, and restart. A loose SIM connection can cause intermittent data issues.

Fix 6: Manually Select Your Network

Android: Settings → Connections → Mobile Networks → Network Operators → Search Networks → Select your carrier manually. This forces your phone to reconnect properly.

Fix 7: Reset Network Settings

⚠️ This will erase all saved WiFi passwords and Bluetooth pairings. Write down your WiFi passwords first.

  • Android: Settings → General Management → Reset → Reset Network Settings
  • iPhone: Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Reset → Reset Network Settings

Fix 8: Contact Your Carrier

If none of the above works, call your carrier's helpline. Your SIM may need to be re-provisioned or there may be an outage in your area.

  • Jazz: 111
  • Zong: 310
  • Telenor: 345
  • Ufone: 333
