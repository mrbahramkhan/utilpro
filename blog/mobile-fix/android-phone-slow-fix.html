Android Phone Running Slow? 10 Easy Fixes (2026)

A slow Android phone is frustrating, but you don't always need to factory reset it. These 10 fixes, tried in order, will speed up almost any Android phone running slow due to clutter, poor optimization, or outdated software.

Fix 1: Clear App Cache

Over time, apps accumulate cache files that can slow down your phone significantly.

1

Go to Settings → Storage

Open Settings and find "Storage" or "Device Care" on Samsung phones.

2

Clear cached data

Tap "Cached Data" and confirm. On newer Android: Settings → Apps → select each heavy app → Storage → Clear Cache.

✅ Focus on heavy apps: Chrome, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok tend to build up the most cache.

Fix 2: Free Up Storage Space

When your phone has less than 1–2 GB of free storage, performance degrades significantly.

  • Delete photos and videos you don't need (or move them to Google Photos)
  • Remove downloaded files from your Downloads folder
  • Uninstall apps you haven't used in months
  • Use Google Files app → Clean tab to find junk files quickly

Fix 3: Limit Background Apps

1

Open Developer Options

Go to Settings → About Phone → tap "Build Number" 7 times to unlock Developer Options.

2

Limit background processes

In Developer Options, find "Background Process Limit" and set it to "At most 2 processes."

Fix 4: Reduce or Disable Animations

This is one of the biggest performance tricks — it makes your phone feel dramatically faster.

1

Open Developer Options

Settings → About Phone → tap Build Number 7 times.

2

Set all animation scales to 0.5x or off

Find "Window animation scale," "Transition animation scale," and "Animator duration scale." Set all to 0.5x or "Animation off."

✅ This single fix makes the biggest visible speed improvement — your phone will feel much snappier immediately.

Fix 5: Update Android & All Apps

  • Android update: Settings → System → System Update → Check for updates
  • App updates: Google Play Store → Profile icon → Manage apps → Update all

Fix 6: Remove Widgets & Live Wallpapers

Widgets on your home screen constantly refresh data in the background. Live wallpapers also consume CPU and battery. Remove unused widgets and switch to a static wallpaper.

Fix 7: Disable Pre-installed Bloatware

Manufacturers pre-install apps you may never use (carrier apps, duplicate apps). You can't always delete them, but you can disable them.

1

Settings → Apps → All apps

Find pre-installed apps you never use (carrier apps, manufacturer apps, duplicate apps).

2

Tap Disable

Tap "Disable" (not Force Stop) to prevent them from running and updating.

Fix 8: Check Battery Health & Performance Mode

Some phones throttle CPU speed when battery health is low. Go to Settings → Battery → Battery Health (on Samsung, it's Settings → Device Care → Battery → Battery Health). If below 80%, the battery itself may be causing slowdown.

Also check that your phone isn't stuck in Power Saving mode, which limits performance.

Fix 9: Boot in Safe Mode to Find Problem Apps

1

Hold Power → Long-press "Power Off"

A "Safe Mode" prompt will appear. Tap OK. Your phone reboots with only system apps running.

2

Test performance in Safe Mode

If your phone is fast in Safe Mode, a third-party app is causing the slowdown. Restart normally and uninstall recently installed apps one by one.

Fix 10: Factory Reset (Last Resort)

⚠️ Back up all data first! Settings → Google → Backup → Back up now. Factory reset erases everything.

If all else fails: Settings → General Management → Reset → Factory Data Reset. This restores your phone to like-new performance.

✅ Most Android slowdowns are fixed by: Clearing cache + freeing storage + reducing animations. Try those three first before anything else.
