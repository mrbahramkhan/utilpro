Exam Preparation Tips

  • Solve at least 5 years of past papers before your exam
  • Focus on "important questions" that appear repeatedly
  • Study from official textbook Punjab Curriculum Authority (PCA) books
  • Watch Sabaq.pk video lectures for difficult topics
  • Create a study schedule 6–8 weeks before exams
  • Practice MCQs daily — they carry 20–25% of exam marks

✅ Pro tip: Join a WhatsApp or Telegram group for your class/board. Students share guess papers, notes, and study strategies during exam season.
