Check your BISE (Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education) exam results online using your roll number. Results for Matric (9th & 10th class) and Intermediate (11th & 12th class) are announced on each board's official website.

⚡ Results season: Matric results are typically announced in July–August, and Inter results in September–October each year.

How to Check Result by Roll Number

1

Find your board's official website

Use the table above or search "[Board name] result 2026" in Google. Always use the official .edu.pk domain.

2

Click "Result" tab

On the homepage, find the "Results" or "Check Result" section.

3

Select exam and year

Choose: Matric/Inter → Annual/Supply → 2026

4

Enter your Roll Number

Type your roll number exactly as printed on your admit card.

5

View and download result

Your detailed result will appear. Print or screenshot it for your records.

Alternative: Check via ilmkidunya or result.pk

Third-party aggregator sites also publish BISE results simultaneously. Popular ones include:

  • ilmkidunya.com — Most popular result checker for all boards
  • result.pk — Clean interface, fast loading
  • educations.pk — Also shows past year results

✅ Tip: Board websites often crash on result day due to traffic. Use ilmkidunya.com as a backup — it handles high traffic better.

⚠️ If your result shows "Result Withheld," contact your school immediately. This usually means fee dues or document issue, not an exam problem.
